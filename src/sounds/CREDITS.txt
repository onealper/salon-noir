Cards and chips: src/sounds/*.wav (31 files)
============================================

Casino Audio 1.1 by Kenney (https://kenney.nl/assets/casino-audio) - CC0 1.0.
See KENNEY-LICENSE.txt.
  card-slide-3/4/7/8, card-place-1..4, card-shove-1..4, card-fan-1, card-shuffle,
  chip-lay-1..3, chips-collide-1..4, chips-handle-1/3/4/6, chips-stack-1/2/4/5/6
  Source used: https://github.com/Daarko/sparkstream-sounds (re-host of the WAV release)

"Card Game sounds" by haeldb (https://opengameart.org/content/card-game-sounds) - CC0 1.0.
  hael-draw
  Source used: https://github.com/novincode/atomcut-library

Changes, all 31: mono 44.1 kHz, 70 Hz high-pass, leading silence trimmed to the onset
(3-4 ms pre-roll) so every sound lands on its frame, tails faded, levels matched (done
for Spader, the game's earlier name). The game adds a small generated room reverb,
random pitch and level variation, and stereo placement by table position at play time.
